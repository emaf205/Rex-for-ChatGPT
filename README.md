## What is REX?

REX is a deliberately bizarre Chrome extension that makes long ChatGPT sessions impossible to ignore.

It does not block ChatGPT. It does not judge you. It simply watches the session timer and interrupts with an increasingly impatient dinosaur.

## How it works

| Session time | REX does | Message |
|---|---|---|
| **15 minutes** | A small, polite interruption | **Still intentional?** |
| **30 minutes** | A much harder warning | **Take a break.** |
| **60 minutes** | Diplomacy ends | **REX IS NOT HAPPY.** |

You can always continue. The point is not to control your time. The point is to make you notice it.

## Why a dinosaur?

Ordinary reminders are easy to dismiss.

A Tyrannosaurus rex breaking into your ChatGPT session is not.

REX turns a forgettable timer notification into an absurd interruption you will actually remember.

## What REX does not do

- It does not block ChatGPT.
- It does not force you to stop.
- It does not require an account.
- It does not send your conversations to the cloud.
- It does not track what you write.
